## Domain family boundary

Conversation/AI, Business Entity, Escalation, Analytics/Forecast, and Customer Web Chat families are approved, but complete domain components/screens are outside P5B. P5B supplies tokens and generic primitives they can compose in later authorized phases. No fake business records or runtime capability are introduced.
